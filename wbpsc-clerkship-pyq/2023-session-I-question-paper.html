Instructions to candidates (as printed in the official booklet)

  1. This booklet consists of 24 pages including the front page, containing 100 questions. Verify the page numbers and the Test Booklet series on each page and bring any discrepancy at once to the notice of the Invigilator.
  2. Answers will have to be given in the OMR Sheet supplied for the purpose.
  3. Before marking the OMR Sheet, fill in the required particulars. Do not fold the OMR Sheet.
  4. All questions are of multiple-choice type. Four probable answers (A), (B), (C) and (D) are given against each question. Choose the correct or the best answer and darken the corresponding circle with a black ball point pen.
  5. One and only one circle is to be fully blackened. Multiple circles or a wrong circle will be treated as a wrong answer. Use of whitener is strictly prohibited.
  6. There will be negative marking for wrong answers — 0.25 mark for each wrong answer.
  7. Blank pages are provided at the end of the booklet for rough work.
  8. The OMR Sheet and the Test Booklet must be handed over to the Invigilator before leaving the examination hall.
