Three questions worth knowing about. Q.15 (“Change the following sentence into an Affirmative one: This story is incomplete.”): the Commission's key marks (A) This story is not incomplete. Many candidates and coaching sources prefer (B) This story is complete., the usual grammatical transformation. Treat the official option (A) as the credited answer. Q.20 (fill in the blanks): official answer (B) can, as, as, on — “A man can live as satisfactorily as he does at present on half his present income.” Q.84 (who is associated with the sarangi): the musically correct answer is (D) Binda Khan, but the Commission credits (C) Buddhadeb Das Gupta (a santoor player). Copy the official answer for the exam, and remember the fact for interviews.

Everything else in the key is consistent with the questions — arithmetic answers were recomputed independently and match.
